TechNova Electronics — Return & Refund Policy (public knowledge base article)

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery in original condition for a
full refund to the original payment method. Opened software and gift cards
are non-refundable. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise
a $5.99 return label fee applies. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business
days of us receiving the returned item.
