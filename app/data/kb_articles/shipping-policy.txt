TechNova Electronics — Shipping Policy (public knowledge base article)

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $35.
Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available for $12.99. We ship to
the US, Canada, and the EU. Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of
dispatch. We are not responsible for delays caused by the carrier once a
package has left our warehouse.
